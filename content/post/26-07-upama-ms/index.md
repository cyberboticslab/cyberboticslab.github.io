Congratulations to [Upama Pant](/author/upama-pant/), who completed her M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at The Ohio State University in July 2026 and joined Tesla.

Upama's work in the Cyberbotics Lab included control and planning for legged robots. She coauthored [Perceptive Variable-Timing Footstep Planning for Humanoid Locomotion on Disconnected Footholds](/publication/xiang-2026-perceptive/). We thank Upama for her contributions and wish her well at Tesla.
