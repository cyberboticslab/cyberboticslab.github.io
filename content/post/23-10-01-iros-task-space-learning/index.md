[Template Model Inspired Task Space Learning for Robust Bipedal Locomotion](/publication/castillo-2023-template/), by Guillermo A. Castillo, Bowen Weng, Shunpeng Yang, Wei Zhang, and Ayonga Hereid, was accepted to **IROS 2023**.

The paper combines a high-level reinforcement-learning planner with a model-based controller, using a reduced-order walking model to design the policy's observations and actions. IROS 2023 took place in Detroit, Michigan, from October 1 to 5, 2023.

[Read the paper and demonstration](/publication/castillo-2023-template/) or visit the [proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS55552.2023.10341263).
