[Time-Varying ALIP Model and Robust Foot-Placement Control for Underactuated Bipedal Robotic Walking on a Swaying Rigid Surface](/publication/gao-2023-time/), by Yuan Gao, Yukai Gong, Victor Paredes, Ayonga Hereid, and Yan Gu, was accepted to the **2023 American Control Conference (ACC)**.

The paper develops a reduced-order model and foot-placement controller that account for horizontal surface motion, with evaluation in planar biped simulation. ACC 2023 took place in San Diego, California, from May 31 to June 2, 2023.

[Read the paper and citation](/publication/gao-2023-time/) or visit the [proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.23919/ACC55779.2023.10156254).
