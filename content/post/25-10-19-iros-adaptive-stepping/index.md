[Adaptive Step Duration for Accurate Foot Placement: Achieving Robust Bipedal Locomotion on Terrains with Restricted Footholds](/publication/xiang-2024-adaptive/), by Zhaoyang Xiang, Victor Paredes, Guillermo A. Castillo, and Ayonga Hereid, was accepted to **IROS 2025**.

The paper jointly adapts step timing and foot placement, with simulation studies on stepping-stone terrain and under external disturbances. IROS 2025 took place in Hangzhou, China, from October 19 to 25, 2025.

[Read the paper and demonstration](/publication/xiang-2024-adaptive/) or visit the [proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS60139.2025.11246171).
