Congratulations to [Victor Paredes](/author/victor-paredes/), who graduated with a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from The Ohio State University in August 2025. Victor is the Cyberbotics Lab's second Ph.D. graduate, following [Guillermo Castillo](/post/24-02-26-guillermo-phd-defense/).

His research in the lab addressed planning and control for bipedal locomotion, including [whole-body task-space control](/publication/paredes-2024-safe/) and [momentum-based walking control](/publication/paredes-2024-moving/). We thank Victor for his contributions and congratulate him on this milestone.
