[On the Comparability and Optimal Aggressiveness of the Adversarial Scenario-Based Safety Testing of Robots](/publication/weng-2023-comparability/), by Bowen Weng, Guillermo A. Castillo, Wei Zhang, and Ayonga Hereid, was accepted to **IEEE Transactions on Robotics** on March 30, 2023.

The paper studies when scenario-based safety-testing methods can be fairly compared and develops a criterion for comparing testing aggressiveness. It includes examples involving bipedal locomotion controllers and vehicle decision-making modules.

The [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2023.3267020) appeared in volume 39, issue 4, pages 3299–3318. Its first-page manuscript history records the acceptance date.
