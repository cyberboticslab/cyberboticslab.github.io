Victor Paredes and Ayonga Hereid deposited [Model-Informed Safe Reinforcement Learning for Bipedal Locomotion via Step-to-Step Prediction](/publication/paredes-2026-model-informed/) on arXiv on September 28, 2026. Their record reports acceptance to IROS 2026.

[The authors' arXiv announcement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34486) provides the acceptance status. This news item is dated to that announcement.

The study evaluates the approach in full-order Digit simulation using MuJoCo. Its safety certificate is derived for the reduced-order model; the results do not establish a general hardware safety guarantee.
