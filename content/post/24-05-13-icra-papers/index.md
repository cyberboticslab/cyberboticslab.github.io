Two papers from the Cyberbotics Lab and collaborators were accepted to **ICRA 2024**, held in Yokohama, Japan, from May 13 to 17, 2024:

- [Data-Driven Latent Space Representation for Robust Bipedal Locomotion Learning](/publication/castillo-2024-data/), by Guillermo A. Castillo, Bowen Weng, Wei Zhang, and Ayonga Hereid, studies compact learned state representations for locomotion. [Proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10610978).
- [Towards Standardized Disturbance Rejection Testing of Legged Robot Locomotion with Linear Impactor: A Preliminary Study, Observations, and Implications](/publication/weng-2024-standardized/), by Bowen Weng, Guillermo A. Castillo, Yun-Seok Kang, and Ayonga Hereid, evaluates repeatable frontal-impact testing of three Digit controllers during walking in place. [Proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10610064).

The linked publication pages include the papers, citations, and demonstrations.
