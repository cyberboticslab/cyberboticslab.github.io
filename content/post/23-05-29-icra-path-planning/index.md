[Safe Bipedal Path Planning via Control Barrier Functions for Polynomial Shape Obstacles Estimated Using Logistic Regression](/publication/peng-2023-safe/), by Chengyang Peng, Octavian Donca, Guillermo Castillo, and Ayonga Hereid, was accepted to **ICRA 2023**.

The work connects obstacle-shape estimation with control barrier functions for bipedal path planning. ICRA 2023 took place in London, United Kingdom, from May 29 to June 2, 2023.

[Read the paper and citation](/publication/peng-2023-safe/) or visit the [proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA48891.2023.10160671).
