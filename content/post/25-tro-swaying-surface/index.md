[Time-Varying Foot Placement Control for Humanoid Walking on Swaying Rigid Surface](/publication/gao-2024-time/), by Yuan Gao, Victor Paredes, Yukai Gong, Zijian He, Ayonga Hereid, and Yan Gu, was accepted for publication in **IEEE Transactions on Robotics** and published in volume 41, pages 5740–5760, in 2025.

The paper develops a foot-placement controller that accounts for the motion of the supporting surface, with evaluation in Digit simulation and hardware experiments on swaying ground.

[Read the paper and demonstration](/publication/gao-2024-time/) or visit the [journal record](https://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2025.3612326).
