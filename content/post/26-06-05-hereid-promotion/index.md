Ohio State's [June 5, 2026 promotion and tenure announcement](https://oaa.osu.edu/news/2026/06/05/promotion-and-tenure-approvals-2026) lists Ayonga Hereid among faculty promoted to Associate Professor with tenure in the College of Engineering. The announcement states that the promotions became effective June 4, 2026.

Prof. Hereid leads the Cyberbotics Lab in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. [Read his lab profile](/author/ayonga-hereid/).
