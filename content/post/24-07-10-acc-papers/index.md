Two papers led by [Victor Paredes](/author/victor-paredes/) were accepted to the **2024 American Control Conference (ACC)**, held in Toronto, Canada, from July 10 to 12, 2024:

- [Safe Whole-Body Task Space Control for Humanoid Robots](/publication/paredes-2024-safe/), with Ayonga Hereid, incorporates control barrier functions into whole-body task-space control. [Proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.23919/ACC60939.2024.10644227).
- [Moving Past Point-Contacts: Extending the ALIP Model to Humanoids with Non-Trivial Feet Using Hierarchical, Full-Body Momentum Control](/publication/paredes-2024-moving/), with Daniel A. Hagen, Samuel W. Chesebrough, Riley Swann, Denis Garagić, and Ayonga Hereid, extends momentum-based walking control to account for finite-sized feet. [Proceedings record](https://doi.org/10.23919/ACC60939.2024.10644609).

The linked publication pages provide manuscripts, citations, and further details.
