## The problem

A powered lower-limb exoskeleton must support walking while balancing the coupled human–robot system. Controller design must account for hybrid contact dynamics and differences between users.

## Our approach

We translate hybrid zero dynamics and trajectory optimization methods from bipedal robots to exoskeletons. Related work studies gait generation, feedback control, and adaptation to model uncertainty.

## Results and experimental setting

The linked ATALANTE studies report dynamic walking experiments with human participants, including walking without crutches in the reported experimental setting. These studies establish controller results under their stated conditions; they do not establish broad clinical efficacy, rehabilitation outcomes, or suitability for every user.
