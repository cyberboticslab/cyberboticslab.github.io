## The problem

Full-body powered exoskeletons couple a person's motion with an actuated mechanical system. Locomotion control must account for balance, contact transitions, and the interaction between the two.

## Research direction

This project explores how hybrid locomotion methods developed for bipedal robots could inform industrial full-body exoskeleton control. Reduced-order walking models, gait generation, and feedback control provide a basis for studying this problem.

## Evidence and scope

Related lab papers establish control methods on bipedal robots and lower-limb exoskeletons. Industrial full-body assistance remains a research direction on this page. Those results do not establish a validated deployment of an industrial full-body exoskeleton or a demonstrated increase in worker productivity.
